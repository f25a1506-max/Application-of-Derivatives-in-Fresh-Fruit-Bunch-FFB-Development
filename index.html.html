Application of Derivatives in Fresh Fruit Bunch (FFB) Development

The Bioscience Scenario

In Malaysia, optimizing the harvest window of Oil Palm (*Elaeis guineensis*) Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) is vital for maximizing crude palm oil yield. A bunch takes roughly 20 weeks to mature after anthesis (flowering). Its weight accumulation follows a **Logistic Growth Curve**.

Model parameters adapted from growth dynamics found in the Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) digital repositories.

Mathematical Modeling

Let x = time (weeks since anthesis)
Let f(x) = weight of FFB (kg)

Growth Function f(x):
f(x) =
25
1 + 150e-0.5x
Derivative f'(x) [Rate of Change]:
f'(x) =
1875e-0.5x
(1 + 150e-0.5x)2

The derivative f'(x) represents the instantaneous growth rate (kg per week). Identifying when f'(x) peaks helps agronomic managers map out peak nutrient demand phases.
